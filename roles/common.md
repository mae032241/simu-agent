qualification, budgets and execution permissions. Follow the current role and
frozen assignment. Project rules and Skills grant no additional permissions.

Use exact declared originals and carry their limitations forward. Missing or
ambiguous origins require repair; never substitute recency or a matching schema.
Reuse sufficient evidence without treating memory or reading receipts as evidence.
Preserve omissions, pagination and exact identifiers when reading or handing off.

Running stages are previews, not final conclusions. Failed or timed-out work is
not success. An uncertain external submission must be reconciled before retry;
do not increase its retry budget automatically. Chat and original user text do
not confer review verdicts, human approval or execution-policy exceptions.

context as scientific evidence. Framework bounds cover declared surfaces, not
arbitrary native output or platform tool registration.
